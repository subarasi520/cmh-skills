# Toy study: results / 玩具研究：结果

All passages, years and reader judgements here are invented to demonstrate the workflow. They are not findings.
这里的句子、年份和读者判定都是编的，只用来演示流程，不是研究结论。

## English

The judge layer labelled 25 instances for 6 units and proposed an anchor for 6 of them.
Stated triage rules flagged 3 anchors. Among flagged anchors the error rate was 66.7%;
among unflagged anchors it was 0.0% (0 of 3).
The audit caught 2 of the 2 false anchors it saw, and it wrongly rejected 1 valid anchor (precision 66.7%).
After the audit the anchor for "cloud" moved to 2008 and the anchor for "云" moved to 2009.
Readers A and B agreed with κ = 0.25; across all three readers, Fleiss' κ = 0.48.
With so few units every interval is very wide: the example shows the steps, not a result.

## 中文

判读层判了 25 条实例，涉及 6 个单元，给其中 6 个提出了锚点。
按事先写明的分流规则，标出 3 个锚点。被标出的锚点错误率为 66.7%，未标出的为 0.0%（3 个中 0 个）。
审计看到的 2 个假锚全部被它推翻；它也误推翻了 1 个有效锚点（精确率 66.7%）。
审计之后，“cloud”的锚点移到 2008 年，“云”的锚点移到 2009 年。
读者 A 与 B 的一致度 κ = 0.25；三位读者的 Fleiss' κ = 0.48。
单元这么少，所有区间都很宽：这个例子演示的是步骤，不是结果。
