# Annotator guide (template)

Adapt this guide to your study, then freeze it with the rest of the protocol.

## Your task

Each row of your sheet shows a passage from a corpus, an expression in it, and a definition
(the "target sense"). For each row, decide whether the expression in this passage is used in
the target sense.

| Answer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Yes: the expression is used in the target sense |
| 2 | No: another sense, or the expression is only part of a longer word or name |
| 3 | Can't tell from this passage |

Write the number in the `label` column. If you answer 2 or 3, a short reason in `note` helps.

## Rules

- Judge only what the passage shows. Do not look the word up, and do not use what you know
  about when a word or sense appeared.
- Work alone. Do not discuss items with other annotators until everyone has returned their sheet.
- Do not use AI tools, search engines or dictionaries for the judgements.
- Do not change the passage, the definition, the order of columns, or the item ids.
- "Can't tell" is a valid answer. A guess is worse than a 3.
- Take breaks. Accuracy drops on long sessions.

## Returning the sheet

Save the file in the same format (CSV, UTF-8) and send it back to the person who sent it.
Keep no copy of other annotators' sheets.
